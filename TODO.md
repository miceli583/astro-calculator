## Bugs (broken functionality)

- [ ] **Polar charts return Porphyry cusps under the requested system's name.** `calcHouses` in `src/lib/ephemeris/client.ts` reads `result.data` and discards `result.flag`. Inside the polar circles sweph sets `flag = −1` for Placidus and Koch and substitutes Porphyry cusps — byte-identical, measured. A caller asking for `placidus` at Tromsø gets Porphyry cusps, a response still labelled `placidus`, and a warning saying they "may be unreliable" (they are exact Porphyry cusps, not unreliable Placidus ones). Same class as F1: sweph signals status through a return code and the wrapper drops it. Our 66.5° constant also disagrees with sweph's true polar-circle boundary — take it from the flag. Three dispositions in `docs/chart-conventions.md` §7 (F5).
- [ ] **A collapsed polar wheel puts every body in house 1.** Above the polar circle, over a window of sidereal time that widens with latitude (0/360 below the circle → 122/360 at Longyearbyen → 176/360 at 89°), Regiomontanus and Campanus cusps collapse onto two points and the first forward arc goes negative. `houseFor` reads a −0.03° house as a 359.97° one, so it swallows the zodiac. Measured end to end: all 13 bodies reported in house 1, with the Moon and Jupiter demonstrably inside the wide house 3. `flag = 0` throughout — the cusps are geometrically exact to 1e-10″, so this is our output shape and ordering assumption, not a sweph bug. Write-up in `docs/chart-conventions.md` §6.2 and §7 (F6).
- [ ] **Sect (day/night) is read off the Sun's house number, so the Part of Fortune depends on the requested house system.** `isDayBirth = sun.house >= 7 && sun.house <= 12` tests "Sun above the horizon" correctly only where houses 7–12 span DSC→ASC. Under `whole_sign` the boundaries are offset from the horizon by the Ascendant's degree within its sign, so ~8% of births on average (up to 16%) are misclassified. Measured (Kolkata 1972): Placidus says day, whole-sign says night, and the Part of Fortune moves **135°** — from nothing but the caller's house-system choice. Sect is a property of the sky, so this is a defect, not a convention difference. Fix: derive it from the ASC/DSC arc directly (two lines, correct under every system). `docs/aspect-conventions.md` §8 (F7).
- [ ] **The Part of Fortune is fabricated when the Sun or Moon is absent.** With a `planets` subset omitting either luminary, the code returns the **Ascendant's longitude** with `isDayBirth: false` — measured identical to the last digit (245.25346052636763), with a populated sign, degree and house. Indistinguishable from a real PoF conjunct the ASC, which is a genuine configuration, so a consumer cannot tell and will render it. Should be `null`. `docs/aspect-conventions.md` §8 (F8).
- [ ] **`/api/v1/transit` uses natal orbs while `/api/v1/transit/natal` uses transit orbs.** Two endpoints answer the same question through two paths with tables 2–2.7× apart. Measured (Diana, 2026-08-12): **54 hits vs 18**, with 36 of the 54 wider than `DEFAULT_TRANSIT_ORBS` (incl. a Sun–Moon opposition at 5.23°). `calculateTransits` also ignores the caller's `orbs` override, since `ASPECT_DEFS` is a module constant. `docs/aspect-conventions.md` §8 (F9).
- [ ] **`/api/v1/transit` hardcodes `applying: false` on every aspect.** The transiting bodies' speeds are on the objects being iterated and the overlay path computes the flag correctly from them. Measured: **0 of 54** applying, against 10 of 18 on `/api/v1/transit/natal`. A blanket `false` is not a conservative default — it is wrong on about half the list, and it is the half that matters, since an applying transit is the one that has not yet peaked. Present-and-always-false is worse than absent: absent is unknown, `false` is a claim. `docs/aspect-conventions.md` §8 (F10).
- [x] **Every chart carries a tautological node–node opposition at orb 0.00.** Fixed — the `true_node`/`south_node` pair is excluded in `computeAspects`; the South Node keeps every other aspect and no other pair is suppressed. Convention promoted to `docs/aspect-conventions.md` §1.7. (F11)
- [ ] **Every birth date before 1800 returns a 500.** `calcPlanet` in `src/lib/ephemeris/client.ts` throws on any non-empty `out.error`, but sweph uses that field for *warnings* too and signals real failure via `flag < 0`. With no `sepl_12.se1` shipped, pre-1800 charts get the warning `SwissEph file 'sepl_12.se1' not found ... using Moshier eph.` and 500. Verified end-to-end: 1799 throws, 1883 and 1950 are fine. Found by the L1 accuracy harness; full write-up and the three candidate dispositions in `docs/accuracy.md` §6 (F1). Chiron is a separate case — sweph genuinely refuses it pre-1800 (F2).
